The process

Scroll through it
01
Week one

A 30-minute conversation

You describe the workflow that frustrates you. We ask about the systems involved and who touches them. If technology isn't the answer, we say so.

02
Weeks two to six, typically

One scoped build

We agree on a single problem worth solving, connect to the systems that hold the data, and build the smallest thing that solves it properly.

03
Ongoing

Measure, then extend

We watch how the solution performs against the workflow it replaced, tune it, and only then talk about what to build next.
